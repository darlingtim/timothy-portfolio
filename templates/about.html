About Timothy Ododo

Technology Mentor, Systems Troubleshooter, and Backend Software Practitioner.

Where Technology Meets People

I operate at the intersection of technical systems, education, and community advocacy. Rather than viewing engineering as an isolated craft, I believe that building software, diagnosing computer networks, and mentoring students are complementary expressions of the same core principle: making complex technology accessible, reliable, and useful for people.

My journey began with deep curiosity about how computer hardware and networks function, which led me to earn the Google IT Support Professional Certificate and master core systems administration, TCP/IP networking, and operating system diagnostics. That foundational curiosity expanded into software engineering, where I specialize in Go (Golang), building clean REST services, containerised microservices, and physical computing solutions with microcontrollers.
